AnkiSync iCloud Updates — ONE TIME IPA REBUILD
=============================================

DOWNLOAD 2 SEPARATE PACKAGES:

A. AnkiSync-iCloud-Updater-iOS-Source.zip
   Upload its contents to your existing private GitHub repository, replacing
   existing iOS source files. Keep the .github/workflows/build-ipa.yml path.
   Run Actions > Build AnkiSync unsigned IPA, sideload the new IPA on BOTH
   iPhone and iPad without uninstalling the existing app first.

B. AnkiSync-iCloud-Updater-PRIVATE-Publisher.zip
   Keep only on your Windows PC. NEVER upload its private .pem signing key to
   GitHub, iCloud Drive, or a shared folder.

AFTER THE ONE-TIME UPGRADE:
1. Open the installed iOS app, Connect iCloud Anki, select iCloud Drive/Anki.
2. For any future HTML/UI update, replace AnkiSync.html in the PRIVATE
   publisher folder, then double-click Publish-Update.cmd on your PC.
3. The script signs HTML and publishes into iCloud Drive/Anki/AnkiSync-Updates.
4. After iCloud sync, iOS AnkiSync shows Update Available; confirm to install.
   No new IPA/GitHub build is needed for HTML/CSS/JS changes.

BEFORE REBUILDING: export .apkg backups of all decks and review progress.
This feature does not itself repair deck synchronization or renew IPA signing.
Read README.md for the full setup and safety notes.
